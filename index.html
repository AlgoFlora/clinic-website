FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the clinic timings?

Morning: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM & Evening: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM. Open Monday to Sunday (all days).

Do I need to book an appointment?

Walk-in patients are welcome, but we recommend booking an appointment for shorter wait times.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept cash, UPI, and major debit/credit cards.

Is parking available?

Yes, there is parking space available near the clinic.

Can I get my reports online?

Yes, we can share basic diagnostic reports via WhatsApp or email upon request.
